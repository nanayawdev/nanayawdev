# DEVS & CREATIVES

**Mission:** Empower African brands with world-class digital experiences that drive growth, inspire confidence, and connect with their audiences globally.

You'll have two divisions under one creative roof:

### Development Division

- Mobile app prototypes
- API integration
- Maintenance & hosting

### Creative Division
- Brand identity & design systems
- UI/UX design
- Motion graphics & 3D visuals
- Social media design
- Product packaging visuals

**Future Services:** Copywriting, Digital marketing, and Product strategy (for startups).
